Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AlfaHr, operating the online clothing and apparel store Prateek Gupta (“we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, stores, and protects customer information when you visit our website or place an order.

Information we collect

We collect only the following customer information when you place an order or contact us: Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Shipping Address, and Order/Payment Reference IDs. This information is collected solely to process orders, arrange delivery, provide customer support, and handle payment verification.

How we use information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Processing and fulfilling your online clothing and dress orders.
  • Arranging shipping and doorstep delivery to your provided shipping address.
  • Verifying payments, reconciling transactions, and processing eligible refunds.
  • Providing responsive customer support and responding to order or payment inquiries.
  • Preventing fraud and complying with applicable Indian tax and legal obligations.

Payments

Online payments are processed through authorized third-party payment gateway providers (such as Paytm). Payment card, UPI, and banking credentials are handled directly on the payment provider’s secure systems in accordance with their security standards and policies. We receive and retain only your Order/Payment Reference ID, transaction status, and payment amount for order confirmation, verification, and refund processing. We never ask customers to share passwords, PINs, CVVs, or OTPs via email, phone, or support messages.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share relevant details strictly on a need-to-know basis with authorized payment gateway partners (for payment processing and verification), courier and logistics partners (to deliver orders to your shipping address), and government or regulatory authorities when required under Indian law.

Data retention and security

We retain customer order and payment reference records only for as long as necessary to complete deliveries, manage returns or refunds, provide support, and satisfy applicable statutory accounting and tax record-keeping requirements under Indian law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, though no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Your choices and rights

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information where applicable under law, or to raise a privacy concern, please contact us at prateekgupta0699@gmail.com. We will review and respond promptly during working hours.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses only essential technical browser functionality required to display pages properly. We do not use third-party advertising pixels or behavioral tracking cookies.

Children

Our online store and services are intended for individuals aged 18 years and older (or minors purchasing with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without guardian consent; if you believe a child has submitted personal details without consent, contact us so we can delete the record.
