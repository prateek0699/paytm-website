Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains order cancellations, returns, and refunds for clothing, apparel, and dresses purchased from Prateek Gupta, operated by AlfaHr.

Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an order before it has been dispatched by contacting us at prateekgupta0699@gmail.com or +91 6307745191 during support hours (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST) with their Order Reference ID. Orders that have already been dispatched cannot be cancelled in transit and will be handled under the returns process after delivery.

Returns and exchanges

If you receive clothing, apparel, or a dress that is damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with your Order Reference ID and details of the issue. Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original condition with tags and packaging intact. Once verified, we will arrange a replacement/exchange or process a refund based on your preference and stock availability.

Refund eligibility

Refunds are issued in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate payments: When a customer is charged more than once for the same order.
  • Failed transactions: When money is debited from the customer’s bank account, card, or wallet, but the order is not confirmed.
  • Eligible order cancellations: When an order is cancelled prior to dispatch, or cancelled by us due to stock unavailability, or when an eligible return of a damaged/defective/incorrect item is approved.

How to request a refund

Contact prateekgupta0699@gmail.com or +91 6307745191 with your Order/Payment Reference ID, registered name and contact details, payment date, and transaction amount. Never share card PINs, CVVs, passwords, or OTPs.

Processing time

Once your cancellation, failed transaction, duplicate charge, or return is verified and approved (typically within 1–2 business days of receiving your request), the approved refund is processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Actual credit timing may vary slightly depending on your bank or payment service provider.

Failed or duplicate payments

If a payment is debited from your account but your order is not confirmed, or if you notice a duplicate charge, please send us your Order/Payment Reference ID, date, and amount. We will verify the transaction status with our payment gateway partner and ensure any confirmed duplicate or unfulfilled debit is refunded to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
